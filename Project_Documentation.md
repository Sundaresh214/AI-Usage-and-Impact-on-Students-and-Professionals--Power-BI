# Project Documentation: AI Usage and Impact on Students and Professionals

## 1. Introduction
AI tools are increasingly used in education and professional environments. This project uses Power BI to explore reported AI usage and perceived impact among students and professionals.

## 2. Problem Statement
Usage patterns and perceived effects may differ between students and professionals. An interactive report helps compare responses and identify trends for further investigation.

## 3. Objectives
- Analyze AI usage among students and professionals.
- Compare patterns across the groups.
- Explore reported use cases, benefits, and challenges where the dataset includes them.
- Communicate findings using interactive visualizations.
